Three reels, one review loop.

Each reel is a short montage built from real screenshots of the installed app, not a literal screen recording. They show the same path a tester follows: see the structure, pick a task, review the agent’s patch and keep working in the editor.

No narration is hidden inside the app: the Change Brief shows files, symbols, cells, checks and risk first. When a model adds a narrative, it is shown separately and labelled as enrichment.
